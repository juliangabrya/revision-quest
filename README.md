# 🎮 Revision Quest

Two fun, **offline** browser games for revising Year 8 exams — built from real knowledge-organiser material. No installs, no logins, no ads. Just open and play.

👉 **Play online:** **https://juliangabrya.github.io/revision-quest/**

## 🏰 HistoryQuest
A learn-then-battle RPG. Study each topic's lesson cards, then beat the boss in a meme-powered quiz. Then sit the **Final Exam** across all topics for a GCSE-style grade and a weakest-first gap report.

**Topics:** Key Terms · Medieval Church · English Civil War · Abolition of Slavery · Ottoman Empire · British Empire · Industrial Revolution

## ⚗️ MadLab
An interactive science sandbox + exam trainer:

- **Reaction Chamber** — mix reagents and watch fizzing, colour changes, precipitates & explosions
- **Pro Lab** — ~120 chemicals, real apparatus (test tube, beaker, flask, **distillation rig**), gas tests, combustion & oxidisers
- **Dissection Bay** — cut open a frog and learn the organs
- **Matter Morpher** — heat/cool molecules through solid → liquid → gas
- **Circuit Lab** — build series & parallel circuits
- **Force Lab** — moments seesaw + calculation practice
- **Exam Arena** — boss-battle quizzes across 11 science topics + a gap report
- **Written Exam** — 1/2/3-mark questions with mark schemes

## How to run locally
Just download and double-click `index.html` (or either game's `.html`). Everything is self-contained — works with no internet.
